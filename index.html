Privacy Policy

Simple, transparent, and built with user privacy first.

1. Data Collection & Usage

Discogs Collection Browser only collects your Discogs username and your personal API key token. This information is used exclusively to connect to Discogs and retrieve your music collection and related data. No other personal data or user metrics are collected, tracked, or stored.

2. Local Storage & Security

Your Discogs credentials stay local to your device. They are used solely to send requests directly to the official Discogs API and are never transmitted to, shared with, or stored on any third-party servers.

3. Data Removal

Because all stored information (your username, API key token, and local database) resides completely on your computer, you retain full control over your data. You can delete your credentials and cached collection at any time by clearing your settings within the application or by uninstalling the application and deleting its local data directory from your device.

4. Third-Party Services

All music and collection data is retrieved directly from Discogs. For information regarding how Discogs processes public account data, please refer to the Discogs Privacy Policy.
